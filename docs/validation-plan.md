# SomnaShift — next evaluation

Status: not run. This plan is a guide for future observation, not research evidence.

Use synthetic records only. Invite outgoing and incoming nurses where possible, explain that this is an independent portfolio concept and obtain permission before recording their screen or comments. Do not collect patient information.

## Ten-minute session

1. Ask the participant to prepare and send Alex’s handoff without describing the controls. Observe whether the unassigned follow-up and named recipient are found.
2. Switch to Jordan. Ask what has happened to the handoff and what has not. Observe the distinction between sent, delivered, viewed and acknowledged.
3. Ask the participant to clarify the expected follow-up time. Observe whether the question’s patient and version are understood.
4. After the answer, ask them to confirm their review. Introduce v2 and ask what their earlier confirmation covers.
5. Open the incoming work scenario. Ask them to find their next task, record a fictional result and then correct an intentionally mistaken completion.
6. Interrupt a half-written form, reload and ask them to continue. Observe whether they distinguish recovered text from submitted information.

## Record observations

For each task, record the first action, points of hesitation, prompts needed, mistaken interpretations and the participant’s explanation in their own words. Distinguish an observed problem from an interpretation or a suggested fix. Keep results anonymous. Do not infer clinical safety from successful use of a fictional prototype.

## Separate device checks

Use a real iPhone with Safari to check keyboard placement, safe areas, form scrolling, orientation changes and closing gestures. Use VoiceOver to inspect reading order, control names, dialog focus and state announcements. Repeat the main task with larger text and reduced motion.

## Decision to revisit

Version-specific acknowledgment adds a deliberate review step. Test whether the difference view makes that step understandable during interruptions, and whether the visible next-follow-up summary helps users resume work without skipping necessary context.
